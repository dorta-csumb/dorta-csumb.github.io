Learning Through Systems

Serious Games teach by turning ideas into systems. Players learn when they make choices, see consequences, and adjust their strategy. This creates understanding that feels earned instead of memorized.

Stealth Assessment

Stealth assessment measures learning through gameplay actions. Instead of quizzes, the game observes how players solve problems and adapts to their skill level.

LLM-PCG

Large language models can generate new challenges, characters, and story paths. This keeps learning fresh and supports personalized experiences.
