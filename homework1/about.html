My Journey

How I Got Here

I became interested in Serious Games after studying how people learn through systems. In CST300, I explored how games can teach complex ideas by letting players experiment, fail, and try again. That research shaped my approach to designing learning experiences.

CyberSurvival

CyberSurvival is a world I designed where networking concepts become survival mechanics. Players learn by navigating threats, managing resources, and discovering how systems interact. It blends fun with real skills.
